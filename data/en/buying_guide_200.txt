200-Point Guide to Buying a Robot Vacuum or Vacuum-and-Mop Robot

1. Core selection principle: First clarify your usage scenario—compact and agile models suit single occupants in small apartments, while households with multiple members and pets should prioritize strong suction power and anti-tangling features.

2. Suction power: For home use, choose models with suction ≥3000Pa; for carpeted areas, select ≥4000Pa. Adjustable suction better adapts to different floor types.

3. Navigation technology: Laser navigation offers precise positioning and strong interference resistance, ideal for complex floor plans; visual navigation is more cost-effective but should be avoided in strong lighting environments.

4. Obstacle avoidance: 3D structured light obstacle detection provides higher accuracy than infrared, effectively avoiding small debris—households with pets should prioritize this feature.

5. Battery runtime: Small apartments (≤80㎡) require ≥60 minutes; large apartments (≥120㎡) should have ≥120 minutes. Models supporting resume cleaning after recharging are more practical.

6. Water tank capacity: Clean-water tanks ≥300ml and dirty-water tanks ≥250ml suit large homes; smaller homes can opt for ~200ml capacity to reduce unit weight.

7. Mop pad type: Electric rotating mop pads offer superior cleaning performance compared to static pressure pads and are essential for stubborn stains. For hardwood floors, choose models with liftable mop pads.

8. Auto-empty function: Automatic dustbin emptying is ideal for convenience seekers. Dust collection bags with capacity ≥2.5L reduce replacement frequency; ensure the design is sealed to prevent dust leakage.

9. Self-cleaning mop: Prioritize models with hot-air drying to prevent mold and odors. Cleaning spin speed ≥150 rpm ensures more thorough cleaning.

10. Brand selection: Top-tier brands (Ecovacs, Roborock) offer better quality control and after-sales service. For lesser-known brands, verify local availability of repair centers.

11. Unit height: If clearance under beds/sofas is ≤8cm, choose a robot with height ≤7.5cm to access hard-to-reach areas.

12. Obstacle climbing: For thresholds ≤2cm high, select robots with climbing ability ≥2cm. Carpeted environments require enhanced drive wheel power to avoid getting stuck.

13. Filtration system: HEPA filters rated ≥H13 effectively capture fine dust and allergens—essential for allergy sufferers. Check if the filter is washable.

14. Main brush design: Anti-tangle main brushes (with silicone strips) suit long-haired individuals or pet owners. Brushless models work well on short-pile carpets and hard floors.

15. Side brush configuration: Dual side brushes provide better edge coverage than single brushes. Models with adjustable rotation speed adapt better to various floor types.

16. APP functionality: Essential features include zone cleaning, no-go zones, and scheduled tasks. Advanced options may include voice control and multi-floor mapping.

17. Network compatibility: Confirm support for dual-band WiFi (2.4GHz/5GHz). For 2.4GHz-only models, ensure full router signal coverage.

18. Noise control: Operating noise ≤55dB is suitable for bedrooms. Carpet mode increases noise; prioritize models with noise-reducing motors.

19. Battery type: Lithium batteries outperform nickel-cadmium in runtime and lifespan. Capacity ≥5200mAh suits large homes; fast charging adds convenience.

20. Charging efficiency: Fast charging (full in ≤2 hours) suits frequent users; standard charging (3–4 hours) offers better value.

21. Material selection: Prefer ABS for durable housing, stainless steel for mop brackets (rust-resistant), and food-grade silicone for seals.

22. Cleaning modes: Essential modes include auto, edge, and spot cleaning. Advanced options like carpet boost, quiet, and detailed cleaning adapt to diverse scenarios.

23. Dirty-water tank design: Float sensors prevent backflow; easy-to-remove tanks simplify cleaning and avoid residual odors.

24. Water tank sealing: Choose double-seal designs to prevent leaks that could damage internal components. Test by tilting the unit during trial use.

25. Voice control: Compatibility with mainstream assistants (e.g., XiaoAI, Tmall Genie) enhances convenience for hands-free operation—verify voice recognition accuracy.

26. Multi-floor mapping: Essential for duplex homes; store ≥3 maps and ensure accurate, drift-free localization when switching floors.

27. Child lock: Households with children should enable child lock to prevent accidental operation—ensure both physical buttons and the app support this feature.

28. After-sales policy: Prioritize models with ≥2-year whole-unit warranty and ≥3-year coverage for core components (motor, battery); after-sales response time should be ≤24 hours.

29. Accessory costs: Verify prices of consumables (filters, mop pads, side brushes). Choose models with widely compatible, cost-effective parts to lower long-term expenses.

30. Weight control: Units weighing ≤5kg are easier to carry manually—convenient for cleaning the charging dock or moving between floors.

31. Floor compatibility: For hardwood floors, choose models with liftable mop pads and adjustable water flow. For tiles, prioritize strong suction combined with rotating mop pads.

32. Pet-specific features: Pet households should focus on anti-hair-tangling and deodorizing functions; consider models with dedicated pet modes for enhanced hair pickup.

33. Fine dust handling: In dusty environments (e.g., post-renovation), choose sealed dustbins with high-efficiency filtration to prevent secondary airborne dust.

34. Ease of operation: For elderly users, prioritize simple physical buttons and intuitive app interfaces to avoid complex setup.

35. Firmware updates: OTA (over-the-air) update support enables navigation algorithm improvements and new features, ensuring evolving user experience.

36. Charging dock design: Charging contacts should have anti-oxidation coating; non-slip base prevents displacement. Auto-return accuracy should be ≥95%.

37. Onboard buttons: Include at least power, return-to-dock, and start-cleaning shortcuts. Buttons should offer clear tactile feedback and waterproofing for easy cleaning.

38. Anti-drop function: Equipped with ≥4 cliff sensors underneath for wide detection range, preventing falls from stairs or elevated surfaces.

39. Drive wheel material: Rubber offers better slip resistance than plastic; textured patterns enhance traction on carpets and wet floors.

40. Caster wheel design: 360° rotation without jamming, plus anti-tangle covers to prevent hair buildup that impairs steering.

41. Cleaning coverage: Refer to real-world tests showing coverage ≥98% to avoid large missed areas; prioritize systematic (planned-path) cleaning models.

42. Water flow adjustment: Offers at least 3 levels—low for hardwood floors, medium for tiles, and high for stubborn stains—to precisely match different cleaning scenarios.

43. Cleaning solution compatibility: Supports adding dedicated cleaning solution; avoid using highly corrosive cleaners. Some models feature automatic cleaning solution mixing.

44. Body waterproofing: Waterproof rating ≥ IPX4, capable of handling damp floors and minor water spills to prevent internal short circuits.

45. Heat dissipation design: Motor heat vents are strategically positioned to avoid blockage by debris, ensuring the motor stays cool during extended operation and prolonging its lifespan.

46. Map editing: Allows manual drawing of cleaning zones, deletion of incorrect maps, and adjustment of no-go zone boundaries to suit complex floor plans.

47. Resume cleaning after recharge: Automatically resumes cleaning from where it left off after recharging when battery runs out, avoiding redundant cleaning and saving time and power.

48. Power consumption control: Energy-saving mode extends runtime by over 30%; use this mode for daily cleaning and switch to max-power mode for tough stains.

49. Exterior design: Body color matches home decor styles; matte finish resists dirt and is easy to clean, avoiding glossy finishes that show fingerprints.

50. Packaging protection: Upon unboxing, check that packaging is intact, all accessories (charging dock, power cable, filters, mop pads, etc.) are included, and there are no signs of impact damage.

51. Real-world testing: If possible, test the unit on-site to evaluate navigation accuracy, obstacle avoidance performance, suction power, and mopping effectiveness.

52. User reviews: Prioritize authentic user feedback, focusing on long-term aspects like battery life, obstacle avoidance, and after-sales service; avoid models with concentrated negative reviews.

53. Price range: For budgets under ¥3,000, choose basic models (with laser navigation and rotating mop); for over ¥5,000, consider models with auto-empty station and hot-air drying.

54. Promotional timing: Major sales events like 618 and Double 11 offer significant discounts; some models allow stacking coupons and gifts to lower purchase cost.

55. Refurbished unit identification: Verify manufacturing date via serial number, inspect screws for signs of tampering, and prioritize official retail channels.

56. Zone cleaning: Supports custom zone sizing, with minimum cleaning area ≤1㎡, ideal for spot-cleaning localized stains.

57. Scheduled cleaning: Set fixed daily cleaning times with weekly recurring options to meet routine cleaning needs.

58. Battery alerts: Dual low-battery notifications via app and indicator light; automatically returns to charge when remaining battery ≥20% to prevent mid-task shutdown.

59. Motor type: Brushless motors are quieter and longer-lasting (≥5,000 hours) than brushed motors; prioritize models with brushless motors.

60. Dustbin capacity: ≥500ml reduces emptying frequency; pet households should opt for ≥600ml; sealed dustbins prevent odor leakage.

61. Filter replacement: Washable filters are more economical and eco-friendly; clean 1–2 times per month. Non-washable filters should be replaced every 3 months.

62. Mop pad replacement: Hook-and-loop design enables easy removal and replacement; choose ultra-fine fiber material for strong water absorption, effective stain removal, and minimal linting.

63. Side brush lifespan: High-quality side brushes last ≥3 months; choose models allowing individual side brush replacement to avoid unnecessary waste.

64. Navigation algorithm: Prioritize models using SLAM algorithms for fast, accurate mapping and reliable navigation in complex layouts without getting lost.

65. Obstacle avoidance range: 3D obstacle detection distance ≥30cm enables early avoidance, reducing collisions and entrapment.

66. Carpet detection: Automatically detects carpets and boosts suction; reverts to normal suction upon leaving carpet to conserve battery.

67. Anti-tangle technology: Main roller brush features automatic hair-cutting function, ideal for long-haired households to avoid manual disassembly and cleaning.

68. Body ports: Includes a charging port for emergency power; some models feature a USB port to temporarily charge other devices.

69. Cleaning logs: App displays cleaning duration, area covered, and path taken, helping users monitor performance and optimize future schedules.

70. Self-diagnostic capability: Automatically detects faults and alerts via app, enabling quick troubleshooting and reducing after-sales communication effort.

71. Remote control: Supports remote start of cleaning tasks; activate while away to return to a freshly cleaned home.

72. Multi-device integration: Compatible with smart home ecosystems (e.g., smart speakers, cameras) for scenario-based cleaning automation.

73. Body dimensions: Width ≤35cm allows easy navigation through furniture gaps; length ≤35cm enhances turning agility.

74. Charging power: Charging power ≥15W enables faster charging; ≤10W is more energy-efficient—choose based on usage needs.

75. Battery cycle life: After ≥500 charge cycles, capacity loss remains ≤20%; high-quality batteries last 2–3 years without replacement.

76. Anti-oxidation treatment: Metal components like charging contacts and screws feature anti-oxidation coating to prevent rust and extend body lifespan.

77. Cleaning mode memory: Remembers the last-used cleaning mode and settings, applying them automatically on next startup without reconfiguration.

78. Quiet operation: Features noise-reducing airflow channels and vibration-dampening foot pads; operating noise ≤50dB, minimizing disruption to rest or work.

79. Wet-mop no-go zones: Allows setting wet-mop restricted areas to prevent mopping on carpets or near wooden floor edges, avoiding moisture damage.
