# 100 Questions About Robot Vacuums
## I. Basics and Technology
### Navigation and Path Planning
1. **How do robot vacuums achieve autonomous navigation?**
- Through LiDAR (LDS), visual sensors (VSLAM), or gyroscope + accelerometer for environmental perception and localization.
2. **Which is better: LDS laser navigation or VSLAM visual navigation?**
- LDS offers higher accuracy and is unaffected by lighting conditions; VSLAM has lower cost and can recognize more object details.
3. **What is dToF navigation technology?**
- Direct Time-of-Flight (dToF) provides more precise distance measurement than traditional LDS, with a detection range up to 10 meters.
4. **Why do some robots get "lost"?**
- Changes in ambient lighting, interference from reflective surfaces, or sensor malfunctions can cause loss of positioning.
5. **How can I improve the mapping accuracy of my robot vacuum?**
- Choose models equipped with LiDAR + AI algorithms, maintain stable lighting conditions, and clean sensors regularly.
### Cleaning System
6. **What does the suction power unit Pa mean for robot vacuums?**
- Pascal (Pa) indicates vacuum suction strength. 2000Pa can pick up mung beans; 2500Pa can effectively clean carpets.
7. **What’s the difference between roller brushes and rubber brushes?**
- Roller brushes offer stronger cleaning but are prone to hair tangling; rubber brushes resist tangling but are slightly less effective at cleaning dust from crevices.
8. **What is the function of side brushes?**
- They sweep dust along walls and corners toward the main suction inlet, improving edge cleaning performance.
9. **What types of dustbin filtration systems are available?**
- Mainly HEPA filters (capture particles as small as 0.3μm), sponge filters, and composite filters.
10. **Why do some models use dual main brushes?**
- Dual counter-rotating main brushes better pick up debris, especially on hard floors.
## II. Product Features
### Sweeping Functions
11. **What types of flooring can robot vacuums clean?**
- Tiles, hardwood floors, and short-pile carpets; long-pile carpets require checking the robot's obstacle-crossing capability.
12. **What is the maximum obstacle-crossing height?**
- Most models handle 1.5–2 cm; high-end models can manage up to 2.5 cm.
13. **How do they detect and avoid carpets?**
- Using ultrasonic sensors or AI vision recognition; some models automatically boost suction when on carpets.
14. **How do you use zone cleaning?**
- Select a specific area on the APP map, and the robot will prioritize cleaning that zone.
15. **What are the different ways to implement virtual walls?**
- Physical magnetic strip virtual walls and APP-based electronic virtual walls.
### Mopping Functions
16. **How effective is mopping on vacuum-and-mop robots?**
- Suitable for daily maintenance; stubborn stains still require manual cleaning and are not as effectively handled as by dedicated floor washers.
17. **What’s the difference between electronically controlled water tanks and gravity-fed water tanks?**
- Electronically controlled tanks precisely regulate water output; gravity-fed tanks rely on natural seepage.
18. **Is vibrating mop technology effective?**
- High-frequency vibration (e.g., 3000 times per minute) enhances stain removal, especially for dried-on dirt.
19. **Is automatic mop washing practical?**
- Very practical—it eliminates manual mop washing, though the base station requires periodic cleaning.
20. **Why do some models lift the roller brush during mopping?**
- To prevent mixing dry and wet debris, keeping the floor dry during mopping.
## III. Smart Interaction
### APP Control
21. **What advanced features do APPs typically offer?**
- Multi-floor map management, cleaning history, consumable reminders, voice control, etc.
22. **How do you set up scheduled cleaning?**
- In the APP, select time, area, and cleaning mode, then save as an automation task.
23. **How many types of no-go zones are there?**
- Three main types: sweeping no-go zones, mopping no-go zones, and virtual walls.
24. **What is the purpose of cleaning records?**
- View historical cleaning area and duration to evaluate cleaning efficiency and consumable lifespan.
25. **How do you switch between multi-floor maps?**
- Save different maps in the APP; the robot automatically identifies the current floor using sensors.
### Voice Interaction
26. **Which voice assistants are supported?**
- Common ones include XiaoAI Assistant, Tmall Genie, Google Assistant, etc.
27. **What operations can be performed via voice control?**
- Basic functions like start/pause cleaning, return to dock, and switch modes.
28. **How do you add custom voice commands?**
- Some models allow setting custom triggers for specific actions in the APP.
## IV. Maintenance and Care
### Daily Maintenance
29. **How often should the dustbin be emptied?**
- Ideally after every use; at minimum, every 2–3 cleaning cycles.
30. **Does the HEPA filter need replacement?**
- Recommended every 3–6 months; washing reduces filtration efficiency.
31. **Do side brushes need replacement?**
- Inspect every 6–12 months; replace if significantly deformed.
32. **How do you clean sensors?**
- Wipe the LiDAR window, cliff sensors, etc., with a dry, soft cloth.
33. **How do you deal with hair tangled around the roller brush?**
- Use the included cleaning tool or carefully cut away with scissors.
### Troubleshooting
34. **What should I do if the robot gets stuck?**
- First power off, check wheels and brushes for obstructions, clear them, then restart.
35. **Why might the robot fail to return to the charging dock?**
- The dock may have been moved, obstacles block its path, or the infrared sensor is dirty.
36. **How to solve the issue of a missed cleaning area?**
- Check if no-go zones have been set; try remapping or manually remote-controlling the robot to clean the missed spot.
37. **How to handle abnormal alarm alerts?**
- Check the error code; common issues will have troubleshooting guidance in the app.
38. **What to do if firmware update fails?**
- Restart the robot, ensure stable network connection, and retry; or contact customer support.
## V. Buying Guide
### Key Selection Factors
39. **Which specifications should I prioritize when purchasing?**
- Navigation type, suction power, battery capacity, and dustbin/water tank capacity.
40. **Which robot vacuum is suitable for small apartments?**
- Basic laser navigation models are sufficient, such as Mi Robot Vacuum-Mop 1C or Roborock T4.
41. **What should I consider for large homes?**
- Choose models with large batteries (>5200mAh), automatic dust collection, or self-cleaning mop pads.
42. **How to choose for households with pets?**
- High suction power (≥2500Pa), rubber roller brush design, and large dustbin capacity.
43. **What to note for homes with many carpets?**
- Confirm carpet recognition and automatic suction boost features; suction power ≥2500Pa.
### Brand Comparison
44. **Main differences between iRobot and domestic Chinese brands?**
- iRobot has mature algorithms but limited features; domestic brands offer richer functionality and better value for money.
45. **Which is better: Ecovacs or Roborock?**
- Ecovacs excels in innovation; Roborock offers better stability and superior algorithms.
46. **What are the advantages of Xiaomi ecosystem products?**
- High cost-performance ratio, seamless integration with Mi Home ecosystem, and low maintenance costs.
47. **Are premium models worth buying?**
- Yes, for users seeking high automation and minimal maintenance.
48. **Is it advisable to buy a used robot vacuum?**
- Not recommended—battery degradation and sensor wear significantly affect performance.
## VI. Technology Development
### Current Technologies
49. **What AI chips are used by mainstream brands?**
- Dedicated AI chips such as Horizon Robotics Journey series, Rockchip, and MediaTek.
50. **How does 3D structured light obstacle avoidance work?**
- It projects an infrared dot pattern to measure object distance and shape, enabling precise obstacle avoidance.
51. **Why can some robots detect pet feces?**
- AI image recognition trained on extensive feces samples, combined with close-range sensors.
52. **How does automatic dust collection work?**
- The base station generates strong suction to transfer debris from the dustbin into a sealed disposable bag.
### Future Trends
53. **How will robot vacuums evolve in the future?**
- Smarter object recognition, robotic arm-assisted cleaning, and fully automated maintenance.
54. **Will robot vacuums replace traditional vacuum cleaners?**
- Unlikely in the short term, but they will take on more daily cleaning tasks.
55. **Are solar-powered robot vacuums feasible?**
- Currently too inefficient; limited to concept products only.
56. **Can robots achieve automatic water changing?**
- Prototype models exist, requiring connection to household plumbing systems.
## VII. Usage Tips
### Daily Use
57. **How to set up the most efficient cleaning schedule?**
- Schedule daily cleaning, and use deep-cleaning mode on weekends.
58. **How to use safely when away from home?**
- Enable remote monitoring, remove items that could cause entanglement, and ensure stable network connectivity.
59. **How to prevent the robot from falling down stairs?**
- Keep cliff sensors clean, or set virtual wall boundaries.
60. **What to note when using across multiple floors?**
- Keep the robot powered on during relocation to avoid map loss.
### Cleaning Performance
61. **How to improve corner cleaning effectiveness?**
- Choose models with dynamic side brushes or square-shaped bodies.
62. **How to avoid water streaks during mopping?**
- Adjust appropriate water flow and use dedicated mopping modes.
63. **How to handle stubborn stains?**
- Pre-clean manually, then run the robot’s deep mopping mode.
64. **How to enhance carpet cleaning performance?**
- Select models with dedicated carpet boost mode.
## VIII. Accessories and Consumables
### Consumable Replacement
65. **How often should filters be replaced?**
- HEPA filters every 3–6 months; sponge filters are washable but still require periodic replacement.
66. **What is the typical lifespan of side brushes?**
- Approximately 6–12 months, depending on usage frequency.
67. **Does the main brush need replacement?**
- Rubber brushes last 1–2 years; bristle brushes should be inspected for wear every 6–12 months.
68. **What types of mop pads are available?**
- Disposable, washable, antimicrobial materials, and more.
69. **Can the dustbin be washed with water?**
- Yes, but it must be completely dry before reuse.
### Accessory Selection
70. **Is it necessary to buy a spare battery?**
- Generally not needed; original batteries typically last 2–3 years.
71. **Can third-party consumables be used?**
- They can be used but may affect performance; original manufacturer consumables are recommended.
72. **How to choose cleaning tools?**
- Select compatible small brushes for easy component cleaning.
## IX. Safety and Privacy
### Data Security
73. **What data does a robot vacuum collect?**
- Home maps, cleaning logs, device information, etc.
74. **How to protect home map privacy?**
- Choose models that store maps locally and disable cloud sync.
75. **Is there a risk of the robot being hacked?**
- Theoretically yes, but mainstream brands implement data encryption measures.
### Operational Safety
76. **Will prolonged charging damage the battery?**
- No, modern lithium-ion batteries include overcharge protection.
77. **Can I touch the robot while it’s operating?**
- Yes, but sudden movement may cause it to lose its positioning.
78. **What should families with children pay attention to?**
- Enable the child lock function to prevent small parts from being accidentally swallowed.
79. **Will pets attack the robot?**
- In rare cases, they might; it's recommended to let your pet get accustomed to the robot first.
## X. Special Scenarios
### Different Environments
80. **What are the recommendations for using in a villa?**
- Choose a model with a large battery and auto-empty dustbin, and set up multi-floor maps.
81. **What should renters pay attention to when using one?**
- Choose a lightweight and portable model, and ensure electrical safety in rental properties.
82. **Are they suitable for commercial spaces?**
- Suitable for small areas; larger spaces require professional commercial-grade models.
### Seasonal Considerations
83. **What should be noted when using in winter?**
- Avoid charging in low-temperature environments (≤0°C), as this may damage the battery.
84. **How should maintenance be handled during the rainy season?**
- Regularly dehumidify to prevent motor moisture damage, and frequently replace mop pads to avoid mold.
85. **Does high summer temperature affect usage?**
- Avoid placing the charging dock in direct sunlight, as high temperatures may impact battery lifespan.

89. **What advantages does it have over traditional housekeeping?**

96. **What stages has the technology gone through?**
- Random bump navigation → gyroscope navigation → laser navigation → AI visual navigation.
