200 Troubleshooting and Repair Guidelines for Robot Vacuums / Vacuum-and-Mop Robots

1. Symptom: Robot does not respond when powered on, and the indicator light is off. Diagnosis: Check whether the power adapter is securely plugged in, whether the power cord is damaged, and whether the battery is properly installed. Repair: Replace the power cord if damaged, reseat the battery, ensure the charging dock is receiving power, and if there is still no response, contact after-sales service to inspect the mainboard.

2. Symptom: Robot shuts down immediately after powering on. Diagnosis: Check whether the battery is fully depleted, whether the robot body is overheated, and whether the battery is swollen. Repair: Fully charge the battery and retry; move the robot to a well-ventilated area to cool down; if the battery is swollen, replace it immediately with an original battery.

3. Symptom: Robot cannot connect to Wi-Fi. Diagnosis: Confirm that both the smartphone and robot are connected to the same 2.4 GHz network, verify that the Wi-Fi password is correct, and check whether the robot’s Wi-Fi module is obstructed. Repair: Restart both the router and the robot, re-enter the Wi-Fi password, and wipe away any dust from the Wi-Fi module area.

4. Symptom: APP cannot control the robot. Diagnosis: Check whether the network connection is stable, whether the APP is updated to the latest version, and whether the robot is in standby mode. Repair: Switch networks, update the APP, restart the robot, and rebind the device.

5. Symptom: Robot frequently collides with furniture during cleaning. Diagnosis: Check whether the obstacle-avoidance camera/sensors are covered with dust or water stains, and whether the bumper strip is stuck. Repair: Wipe the obstacle-avoidance components with a dry cloth, gently press the bumper strip to confirm it rebounds normally, and clear surrounding obstacles.

6. Symptom: Robot does not move or moves very slowly. Diagnosis: Check whether the drive wheels or caster wheels are tangled with hair or debris, whether the wheel assembly is jammed, and whether the floor surface is too slippery. Repair: Remove debris entangled in the wheels, apply a small amount of dedicated lubricant, and test on a dry, non-slippery surface.

7. Symptom: Suction power has significantly decreased. Diagnosis: Check whether the dustbin, filter, or air duct is clogged, whether the roller brush is tangled with debris, and whether the suction inlet is blocked. Repair: Clean each potentially clogged component one by one, cut away hair wrapped around the roller brush, and ensure the suction inlet is free of obstructions.

8. Symptom: Roller brush does not rotate. Diagnosis: Check whether the roller brush is jammed by foreign objects, whether the motor wiring is loose, and whether the roller brush motor has failed. Repair: Clear debris from the roller brush, reinstall it properly, and if it still does not rotate, contact after-sales service to inspect the motor.

9. Symptom: Side brush does not rotate. Diagnosis: Check whether the side brush is properly installed, whether its shaft is tangled with hair, and whether the side brush motor is malfunctioning. Repair: Reinstall the side brush, clean debris from the shaft, and if the motor emits abnormal noise, replace the side brush motor.

10. Symptom: Robot cannot find the charging dock. Diagnosis: Check whether there are obstructions around the charging dock, whether the return-to-dock sensors are dusty, and whether the charging dock’s infrared signal is functioning normally. Repair: Clear obstacles within 3 meters of the charging dock, wipe the return-to-dock sensors, and reposition the charging dock.

11. Symptom: Charging indicator light blinks and the robot fails to charge normally. Diagnosis: Check whether the charging contacts are oxidized or dusty, whether the power adapter is faulty, and whether the battery is damaged. Repair: Wipe the charging contacts with a dry cloth, replace the power adapter, and if the battery is damaged, replace it.

12. Symptom: Battery runtime has drastically decreased. Diagnosis: Check the battery’s usage duration, whether overcharging or deep discharging has occurred, and whether the battery is swollen. Repair: Avoid overcharging or deep discharging, perform periodic full charge/discharge cycles to recalibrate, and replace the original battery if runtime drops below 50% of original capacity.

13. Symptom: Incomplete or distorted map creation. Diagnosis: Check whether the environment contains reflective surfaces, whether there are too many moving obstacles, and whether sensors are malfunctioning. Repair: Cover reflective objects, remove moving obstacles, delete the old map, and rebuild the map.

14. Symptom: Robot frequently pauses during cleaning. Diagnosis: Check whether the dustbin is full, whether battery level is low, and whether sensors are obstructed. Repair: Empty the dustbin, fully charge the battery, and wipe dust from sensor surfaces.

15. Symptom: Water tank leaks during mopping. Diagnosis: Check whether the water tank cap is tightly closed, whether the sealing ring is aged or detached, and whether the water outlet tube is clogged. Repair: Tighten the water tank cap, replace the aged sealing ring, and unclog the water outlet tube.

16. Symptom: No water dispensed during mopping. Diagnosis: Check whether the water tank contains water, whether the water flow setting is at minimum, and whether the water outlet tube is blocked. Repair: Refill the tank with clean water, increase the water flow setting, and gently unclog the outlet tube using a toothpick.

17. Symptom: Electric mop pad does not rotate. Diagnosis: Check whether the mop pad is tangled with debris, whether the mop motor is properly installed, and whether the motor wiring is loose. Repair: Clear debris from the mop pad, reinstall the mop motor, and contact after-sales service to inspect wiring issues.

18. Symptom: Automatic mop-washing function fails. Diagnosis: Check whether the base station’s clean-water tank has water, whether the dirty-water tank is full, and whether the cleaning brush is clogged. Repair: Add clean water, empty the dirty-water tank, and remove debris entangled in the cleaning brush.

19. Symptom: Robot emits a 'beep-beep-beep' alarm sound. Diagnosis: Check the indicator light error code on the robot body and the fault alert in the APP to identify the affected component (e.g., dustbin, filter, battery). Repair: Clean the indicated component as prompted and restart the robot.

20. Symptom: Cliff detection fails, posing a fall risk. Diagnosis: Check whether the cliff sensors on the bottom are dusty or damaged. Repair: Wipe the sensors clean, test the robot on a flat surface, and if sensors are damaged, contact after-sales service for replacement.

21. Symptom: Robot follows a chaotic, irregular cleaning path. Diagnosis: Check whether systematic (planned) cleaning mode is enabled, whether the map is complete, and whether new obstacles have been introduced. Repair: Enable planned cleaning mode, rebuild the map, and remove new obstacles.

22. Symptom: Unable to select zones for zone cleaning. Diagnosis: Check whether mapping was successful, whether the APP is lagging, and whether zone settings were accidentally deleted. Repair: Rebuild the map, restart the APP, and reconfigure zone settings.

23. Symptom: Robot enters areas marked as no-go zones. Diagnosis: Check whether the no-go zone boundaries were accurately set, whether the robot has recognized an updated map, and whether the temporary no-go zone disable feature is active. Repair: Adjust the no-go zone boundaries, ensure the robot recognizes the current map, and disable the temporary override function.

24. Fault symptom: Robot powers off immediately after fully charging; Diagnosis: Check if the battery is swollen and whether the mainboard's power management module is faulty; Repair: Replace the swollen battery and contact after-sales service to inspect the mainboard.

25. Fault symptom: Robot body overheats severely and emits an unusual odor; Diagnosis: Check if the motor is overloaded, if the battery is short-circuited, or if the air duct is blocked; Repair: Immediately power off and allow to cool down, clean the air duct, and contact after-sales service to inspect the motor and battery.
